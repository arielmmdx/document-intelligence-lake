# 05 — Tips: take-home write-up (skip if you are live only)

Stop at 6 hours. Structure that usually scores well:

1. **Critique** of the naive design (generated Python, first 20 pages, one process, crawler as model).  
2. **Airflow DAG + Python helpers** (docstrings, imports, EC2 vs ECS vs Glue). LLM sample vs full file. Who writes silver.  
3. **Work units**: 40-page PDF, 10,000-page PDF, huge Excel, SQLite.  
4. **Recipe vs exec**. Excel/Word sample then scale.  
5. **Landing / bronze / silver / gold** and publish.  
6. **Serving and security** (SSO+MFA, roles, PII).  
7. **SLA and cost** (order of magnitude, p95 vs p99).  
8. **What you skip in v1**, and any AI tools you used.

You may attach mermaid. Naming every AWS service is not a substitute for the 10,000-page story.
