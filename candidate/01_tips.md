# 01 — Tips: frame the problem well

These tips raise the quality of your answer. The problem itself is in [README.md](README.md).

- Name the **unit of done**: one file, one page, one customer day? A 10,000-page PDF that is 90% complete is not “done” if the business needs every claim form.
- Give **two SLAs**: typical (hundreds of pages) vs worst case (10,000 pages). It is senior to say the tail is slower and costs more.
- Separate **landing** (keep the original) from **tables** (what analysts query). Do not mix them in one bucket story.
- Say how you know silver is **safe to read** (a publish step), not “the job returned 200.”
- Tenant: customer A never lists customer B’s files. Mention that early.
